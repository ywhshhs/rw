# AE — ARMORED EXPERIMENTALS: Full Roster & Balance Design

**Doctrine:** ground army, every unit has ONE job, no jack-of-all-trades.
**Mass-production rule:** any unit that is cheap AND cost-efficient 1v1 is banned.
Cheap units lose to their counter even at 1.5:1 cost ratio; expensive units are
strictly weaker than two of the tier below at equal cost — except in combined arms.

## Counters (rock-paper-scissors)
Skirmisher swarms > Rainmaker/Juggernaut → Sentry/Skycutter deny air → Huntsman
snipes heavies → Aegis + Medic hold the line → Colossus/Behemoth break sieges →
Thunderhead outranges everything → Ranger spots it all first.
